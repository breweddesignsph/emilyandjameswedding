Our Story

How We Got Here

The Dinner Party, 2020

Emily and James met at a small dinner party thrown by mutual friends. James noticed her the moment she walked in — though he'll be the first to admit he spent a nervous twenty minutes talking to everyone else in the room over a shared bottle of wine before he worked up the courage to say hello. Emily still teases him about it to this day.

Big Sur, Three Years Later

Three years after that first dinner party, James proposed at Big Sur. There was no elaborate setup, no hidden photographer — just the two of them, the sound of the ocean, and a visibly nervous James fumbling for the right words. Emily said yes before he even finished the question.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What time should we arrive? +

Please plan to be seated by 4:15 PM so the ceremony can begin promptly at 4:30 PM.

Is there parking available at the venue? +

Yes, complimentary on-site parking is available. A valet team will be on hand to help direct guests.

Can I bring a plus one? +

Your invitation and RSVP form will indicate the number of seats reserved in your honor. Please reach out to us directly if you have questions about your invite.

Are children welcome? +

While we love your little ones, our celebration is an adults-only evening, with the exception of immediate family.

What happens if it rains? +

The Willow Estate has a beautiful covered pavilion as a backup ceremony location, so the celebration will go on rain or shine.

Is the ceremony unplugged? +

Yes — we kindly ask that phones and cameras stay put away during the ceremony so everyone can be present. Our photographer will capture every moment, and we'll share photos after the wedding.

Will transportation be provided? +

A shuttle will run between our recommended hotels and the venue throughout the evening. Details will be shared closer to the date.

By when should I RSVP? +

We kindly ask that you RSVP using the form on this site by September 15th, 2026.

Who should I contact with other questions? +

Feel free to reach out to either of us directly, or contact our wedding coordinator, who is happy to help with any details.
